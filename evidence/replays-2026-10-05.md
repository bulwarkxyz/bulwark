# Replay study: resting stage orders, settings sweep, ordinary days (mark prices)

Run 2026-10-05T12:17Z. Cells: share of the 10,000 USDC kept at the end of the window, or L (liquidated).

- **account:** A cross long on the xyz dex, opened at the first mark of each window, with 10,000 USDC of equity and nothing else in the account
- **exampleSettings:** Example settings chosen for the test (not product defaults): Stage 1: buffer below 2× → trim until the buffer is back at 3×; Stage 2: buffer below 1.5× → trim until the buffer is back at 2.5×; Stage 3: buffer below 1.2× → close the position. Except in the repeat comparison, stages act every time the line is crossed (how policies behaved before the choice existed)
- **repeat:** Repeat comparison: every stage set to "every crossing" or to "once per breach" (acts again only after the market recovers to where it was when the stage acted)
- **sweep:** Every combination of three line sets and three trim sizes; each is an example chosen for the test
- **setups:** server = Server only (as first published); current = Current guard: server + resting backstop; resting = Proposed: stages resting on the exchange, fills 1% worse than the mark; restingSafe = Proposed, lower stages priced as if earlier ones fill 5% worse; fills 1% worse; resting3 = Proposed, fills 3% worse than the mark
- **serverLate:** The server's orders and its re-placing of resting orders reach the exchange 0, 1, 5 minutes after the decision; resting orders already on the exchange fire on the mark regardless
- **serverOrderFills:** IOC at the worst price a 1% slippage limit allows; a late IOC fills only if the mark is still within its limit; retries as in the guard
- **backstopFills:** A stop with a limit at the user’s slippage: once the mark crosses the trigger it rests as a limit and fills at that limit only when the mark is within it
- **stageTriggerFills:** Market triggers: fill at the mark of the first round at or past the trigger, made 1% (or 3%) worse; no fill if that is more than 10% worse than the trigger (Hyperliquid’s market-trigger tolerance)
- **stageTriggerPlacement:** Placed from the account before the window starts; re-placed by the server (late as above) after any fill or latch change; each stage’s trigger price holds other positions still
- **fees:** 4.5 bps per fill, assumed
- **notModelled:** Funding, order-book depth and queue position, partial fills, Hyperliquid outages, other traders reacting, open-order and rate limits
