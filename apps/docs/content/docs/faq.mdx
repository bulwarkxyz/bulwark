### Can the guard stop every liquidation?

No. A price can gap past every stage before any order fills. The guard lowers the chance and the damage; it cannot remove the risk.

### What happens if your servers are down?

Reduce-only stop orders already rest on Hyperliquid at your lowest line and trigger on the exchange's mark price without us.

### Who decides the numbers in my rules?

You do. The app and the AI never suggest limits.

### Can Bulwark take my funds?

No. Hyperliquid does not let agent keys withdraw, and our engine only sends reduce-only orders and moves USDC between your own balances. Your guard key is a non-exportable key in AWS KMS: no one can copy it. If our signing service were compromised, someone could have it sign trades on your account while they controlled it, but could not withdraw. See [Key storage](/key-storage).

### Which AI does the translating?

An OpenAI model (GPT-6.1 Sol). It sees your sentence, the market list and your current rules, and nothing else; the request is sent with storage off. It has no keys and no tools. See [Privacy](/privacy).

### Is it live?

It is a testnet preview today, with no fees. Mainnet follows after a small canary run on our own accounts.
