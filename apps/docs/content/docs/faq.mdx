### Can the guard stop every liquidation?

No. A price can gap past every stage before any order fills. The guard lowers the chance and the damage; it cannot remove the risk.

### What happens if your servers are down?

Reduce-only stop orders already rest on Hyperliquid at your lowest line and trigger on the exchange's mark price without us.

### Who decides the numbers in my rules?

You do. The app and the AI never suggest limits.

### Can Bulwark take my funds?

No. Hyperliquid does not let agent keys withdraw, and our engine only sends reduce-only orders and moves USDC between your own balances. Your guard key is stored encrypted on our server: if our server were compromised, someone could place trades on your account, but could not withdraw. Hardware-backed key storage is planned.

### Which AI does the translating?

Claude, by Anthropic. It sees your sentence, the market list and your current rules, and nothing else.

### Is it live?

It is a testnet preview today, with no fees. Mainnet follows after a small canary run on our own accounts.
