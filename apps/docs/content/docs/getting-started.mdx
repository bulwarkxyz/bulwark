---
title: Getting started
description: The setup steps, in order. Each signature is shown in your wallet; none of them moves your funds.
---

<Steps>

<Step>
### Connect and sign in

Connect the wallet you trade with on Hyperliquid and sign the sign-in message. Signing in proves you own the address and authorises nothing.
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm where you are

Enter the country you live in and your citizenship. Bulwark also checks your connection's country and applies the strictest of the three. See [Regions](/regions).
</Step>

<Step>
### Fund your Hyperliquid account

Bulwark trades from your own Hyperliquid account. Deposit USDC there the usual way; nothing is held by Bulwark.
</Step>

<Step>
### Create and approve your guard key

Bulwark creates a guard key for you in AWS KMS, where its private key cannot be exported (see [Key storage](/key-storage)). You approve it on Hyperliquid as a named agent (`bulwark-guard`), optionally with an expiry. Hyperliquid lets agents trade but never withdraw.
</Step>

<Step>
### Approve a trading key (optional)

For your own orders, the app can create a separate key that stays in your browser, so you don't sign every order in your wallet. Like every Hyperliquid agent, it cannot withdraw.
</Step>

<Step>
### Write and sign your rules

Set your buffer lines and what happens at each, or write a rule in plain words and let the AI translator turn it into an exact rule. Every number is one you type. You sign the policy in your wallet; from then on the guard runs it.
</Step>

</Steps>
