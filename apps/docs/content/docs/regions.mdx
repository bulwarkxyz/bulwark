---
title: Regions
description: Where Bulwark is available, and how that is checked.
---

## Not available

- **United States** and its territories (Puerto Rico, Guam, US Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands, US Minor Outlying Islands)
- **Canada**
- **United Kingdom**
- **Russia** and **Belarus**
- **Sanctioned regions:** Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Myanmar, and the Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine

## Trading and alerts only

In the **EU and EEA** you can trade and receive alerts, but the automatic guard is off.

## How it is checked

Bulwark takes the strictest of three signals: the country your connection comes from, the country you say you live in, and your citizenship. If any of the three is a country where Bulwark is not available, access is refused; if any is in the EU or EEA, the automatic guard is off.

The guard checks again before every action, using the latest country your connection came from. If you connect from a country where automatic action is off, the guard switches to alerts only from then on.
