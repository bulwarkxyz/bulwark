---
title: Fees
description: One flat fee, shown before you confirm.
---

| | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Trading** | 3 basis points (0.03%) on orders you place through Bulwark, collected through a Hyperliquid [builder code](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/trading/builder-codes). Shown before you confirm |
| **Guard** | Included. Guard orders carry the same 3 bps. No subscription |
| **Your approval** | You approve a maximum of 0.05% for Bulwark's builder code on Hyperliquid and can lower it to zero at any time |
| **Exchange fees** | Hyperliquid's and the market's own trading fees apply as usual, on top |

<Callout type="info" title="Testnet preview">
  No Bulwark fee is charged during the testnet preview.
</Callout>
